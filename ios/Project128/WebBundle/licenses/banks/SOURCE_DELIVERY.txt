The combined Project128 GS bank includes GeneralUser GS data and eight TimGM6mb alternates. TimGM6mb is GPL-2.0-only. The preferred editable combined source is supplied alongside the source-project ZIP as:

  Project128_GS_Editable_Source.sf2
  32,542,290 bytes
  SHA256 4e8194a30b2364d7e40eda8583b6acd91ffb09e8ccfec3dc74807c1c67e9a2a2

This is the complete combined source before Vorbis conversion, including instrument/sample structures. It is separate from the below20MB app-source ZIP to avoid duplicating the bank in that archive. It is not required to run the player.

When redistributing the compressed bank, also make the editable source and included license/provenance notices available alongside it, with equivalent access. This project does not claim that a build script or third-party URL alone constitutes a GPL written source offer. No three-year source-delivery promise on another person's behalf is being made.

tools/prepare-bank.mjs documents reproducible source acquisition and can write --source-output. PROVENANCE.json records the original sources, exact hashes and measured conversion differences. GeneralUser GS's original license/notice remains included.
